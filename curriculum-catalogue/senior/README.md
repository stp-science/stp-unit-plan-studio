# Senior curriculum references

60 user-supplied Ministry PDFs, October 2026, extracted as page-numbered text. Original filenames, hashes, page counts, subject ranges and status are retained. The catalogue is a supplied subset, not all senior subjects.

Fifty teaching-content documents are proposed curriculum statements, eight are supporting infosheets, one is a supporting document, and Outdoor Education is explicitly a consultation draft. No source is presented as a junior curriculum requirement or a final assessment specification. The junior planner may receive relevant senior teaching-content references only for connections to future learning; infosheets, supporting documents and consultation drafts are excluded from automatic progression retrieval.

Full PDF originals, images and logos are not included in the website; only attributed text derivatives are stored. Originals remain unchanged.
