# Junior Ministry curriculum catalogue

All eight Years 0–10 NZC learning areas are represented by supplied PDFs. Five language teaching sequences are included: Spanish, Chinese (Mandarin), French, Japanese and Te Reo Māori. Other languages are not claimed complete. Supporting PE activity examples, the languages overview and conceptual map are separate reference types, not substitutes for teaching sequences.

Science (August 2026) and Arts (September 2026) duplicate uploads match the earlier PDF hashes and do not create duplicate catalogue entries. English and Mathematics and Statistics use the supplied 2025 PDFs; earlier HTML capture files are retained as archival extraction records, not mixed into the PDF source.

Language progression uses the documents’ Novice 1/2/3 stages rather than a fixed stage-to-year mapping. Source filenames, hashes, versions and physical page numbers are retained. Source text is attributed to the Ministry of Education under its applicable copyright notice; no logos, images or website design are reproduced.

Senior sources live under `senior/` and may inform future-learning connections only. They never fill junior curriculum statement gaps.
